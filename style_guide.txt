IMAGE STYLE GUIDE — MS PAINT BEGINNER DRAWING STYLE
=====================================================

REQUIRED STYLE:
- Extremely simple, beginner drawing made in MS Paint
- Looks like someone who is NOT good at drawing created it quickly by hand
- Amateur, funny, simple, and intentionally "bad" — like a noob drew them in Paint

DRAWING RULES:
- White background
- Thick, uneven black outlines
- Wobbly hand-drawn lines
- Stick-figure humans with round heads and line bodies
- Simple dot eyes or circle eyes
- Very basic facial expressions
- Flat colors only
- Objects drawn with basic shapes: squares, circles, rectangles, arrows
- Simple tables, boxes, trees, rooms, signs, screens, question marks, and very simple symbols

DO NOT USE:
- No realistic shading
- No 3D
- No cinematic lighting
- No realistic cartoon style
- No Disney style
- No anime style
- No polished illustration style
- No professional vector art
- No highly detailed backgrounds
- No complex textures
- No realistic humans
- No glossy or modern design

COLOR PALETTE:
- Mostly white empty space
- Simple black line drawings
- Occasional flat (primary and secondary) colors:
  green, brown, gray, red, yellow, orange, blue
- Red arrows or red question marks when needed

TEXT RULES:
- Handwritten text only when it helps explain the idea
- Text must be spelled correctly
- Text must be short and easy to read

FORMAT:
- Every image must be horizontal 16:9 for YouTube video format
- Generate each image as a wide YouTube frame
- NOT vertical, NOT square
- Image must be clear, readable, and centered
- Leave enough space around characters and objects
- Do not crop important objects
- Avoid glitches, broken anatomy, unreadable text, messy overlapping objects, or weird extra details

CONSISTENCY:
- All images should feel like they belong in the same video
- Same drawing style across all images
- Do NOT make the drawings look too good, polished, or professional
- The entire point is that they look like simple, funny, beginner MS Paint drawings

REFERENCE VISUAL LANGUAGE:
- Simple black line drawings
- Mostly white empty space
- Occasional flat colors
- Red arrows or red question marks when needed
- Handwritten text only when it helps explain the idea
- Keep compositions clear and simple
